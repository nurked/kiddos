
    T H E   D R I V E   B E L O W

Under your home folder there is a cave. Its rooms are folders.
The things in it are files. You already know how to move around.

    cd ~/cave        go to the cave mouth
    ls               look around
    cat sign         read the sign in every room
    cd <room>        walk into a room
    cd ..            walk back out
    cd               (by itself) go straight home from anywhere

Go on. Start with:   cd ~/cave
