# adventure
> a cave where rooms are folders and things are files

## WHAT IT DOES
`play adventure` builds a cave in ~/cave and tells you how to enter. Every
room has a `sign`. Read it with `cat sign`. Exits are folders: `ls` shows
them, `cd` takes you through, `cd ..` brings you back.

## TRY THIS
```
play adventure
cd ~/cave
cat sign
```
